# VoiceNotes Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

VoiceNotes does not require a VoiceNotes account. VoiceNotes does not include advertising or analytics, and it does not send personal data to a server operated by VoiceNotes.

## Information stored on your device

VoiceNotes stores notes, recordings, mood entries, photos, settings, and transcription results on your device. You control this information. You can remove it in the app or by deleting the app.

## Bluetooth accessories

VoiceNotes uses Bluetooth to find, pair with, and exchange data with compatible VoiceNote recorder hardware. This communication is used only to provide the features that you request.

## Microphone, camera, and photos

VoiceNotes uses the microphone when you choose to record audio. It uses the camera or photo library only when you choose to add an image. VoiceNotes does not use these permissions for advertising or tracking.

## Optional transcription

Transcription is optional. If you enable it and provide your own Soniox API key, VoiceNotes sends the selected audio recording directly to Soniox for transcription. Soniox processes this information under your Soniox account and Soniox's terms and privacy practices. VoiceNotes stores your Soniox API key in the iPhone Keychain.

## Tracking and sale of data

VoiceNotes does not track you across apps or websites. VoiceNotes does not sell personal information.

## Changes

This policy can change when the app's features change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the [VoiceNotes GitHub repository](https://github.com/ntna141/VoiceNotes/issues).
